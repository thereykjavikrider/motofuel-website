Support

MotoFuel is built by one rider in Reykjavík. There's no support queue and no ticket bot — an email reaches me directly, and you'll get a real reply.

Frequently asked questions

Where is my data stored?
On your iPhone, in the app. There's no account, no sign-up, and nothing is uploaded to a server. Your riding data never leaves your device unless you export it yourself.
How do I export my data?
Settings → Export. You can export as CSV or JSON, per bike or all bikes.
Which countries have live fuel prices?
Iceland, via Gasvaktin. More countries are planned.
What languages does MotoFuel support?
English, Icelandic, and Danish.
What iOS version do I need?
iOS 16.2 or later.
Does MotoFuel track my location?
Only while you have a ride running, to record your route. Location is used on-device and stored with your ride. You can use the whole app without ever starting a ride.
How do I report a bug or request a feature?
Email — include your bike, what you did, and what happened. Bug reports from riders are how this app improves.
